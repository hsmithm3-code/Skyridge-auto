About

Skyridge is led by Hamilton Smith, a lifelong enthusiast whose love of cars started with his first: a yellow Volvo 850R.

Hamilton has raced in the Skip Barber National and Eastern Region series, won an MX-5 race at Lime Rock, and has been sim racing for more than twenty years. He knows what separates a good car from a great one.
